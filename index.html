🔄 Data Sources & Updates

This system tracks Tariff, Trade Remedies, Export Control, AEO / Enterprise Management, Customs Supervision, Bonded / FTZ, Export Tax Refund, Inspection & Quarantine, and FX Management regulations.

Data Sources:

  • GACC Announcements (海关总署公告专栏)
  • Gov.cn / SC Policy Library (中国政府网/国务院政策文件库)
  • TCTC Policy Releases (财政部关税税则委员会政策发布专栏)
  • MOFCOM Policy Releases (商务部政策发布)
  • STA Tax Regulations Database (国家税务总局税收法规库)
  • SAFE Policy & Regulations (国家外汇管理局政策法规)

⚙️ Update mechanism: GitHub Actions automatically scrapes official government websites every 6 hours (no manual intervention needed, runs even when your computer is offline). A local AI automation reviews newly added entries daily at 09:30 — fixing dates, normalizing titles/document numbers, summarizing descriptions, and converting pending → effective status. Click the 🤖 button in the bottom bar to monitor the latest scraper results.

How to get a PAT:

3. Select scope: public_repo
